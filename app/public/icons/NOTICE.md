external icon CDN. Product names and marks identify the technologies used in the
projects; their respective owners retain their trademark rights.

## Custom JPA monogram

`jpa.svg` is an original, locally drawn text monogram for this portfolio. It is
not an official JPA logo or an external brand asset. The adjacent `JPA` label
identifies the technology; the image is decorative.
